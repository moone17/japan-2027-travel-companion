❄️ Winter travel rules

  • Keep passport, medication, valuables and one change of clothes in your day bag.
  • Do not rely on the last train or last shuttle of the day.
  • Use grippy waterproof shoes and detachable anti-slip cleats for icy paths.
  • For the Niseko + Lake Toya shoot, follow the photographer's road-safety decision.
  • Keep one main activity per day; drop optional stops when tired or weather turns.
